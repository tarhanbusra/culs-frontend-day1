    Herb care tips

    Common questions about keeping your herbs happy.

    How often should I water my herbs?

    Most kitchen herbs prefer soil that dries out slightly between waterings. Stick your finger about 2 cm into the soil — if it feels dry, water thoroughly. Overwatering is the most common mistake with pot-grown herbs.

    Do herbs need full sun?

    Mediterranean herbs such as basil, rosemary, and thyme thrive in at least six hours of direct sunlight. Mint and parsley tolerate partial shade well and can scorch in intense afternoon sun.

    When is the best time to harvest?

    Harvest in the morning after the dew has dried but before the heat of midday. This is when the essential oils — responsible for flavour and aroma — are most concentrated. Never remove more than one-third of the plant at once.

    Can I grow herbs indoors?

    Yes! Place pots on a south- or west-facing windowsill for maximum light. Rotate the pot a quarter-turn every few days so all sides receive equal sun. A grow-light set to 14–16 hours per day works well in darker rooms.
